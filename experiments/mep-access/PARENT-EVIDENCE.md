# MEP replacement-access planning: parent evidence screen

Observed 2026-09-18. This is a potential bid-preparation/service integration, not an operational rigging tool or a pursuit judgment.

## Costly paid workflow

- [Moore's ExxonMobil chilled-water project](https://www.mooreusa.com/projects/empr-chilled-water-plant/) reports a $2.2 million total contract. The chiller exceeded existing openings, requiring a planned wall opening and specialized positioning. The total covers the complete HVAC project, not geometric planning and not an avoidable loss.
- [PSC's chiller replacement](https://pscind.com/projects/chiller-removal-and-replacement) reports 866 estimated man-hours for the full job, constrained parking-garage access, partial equipment disassembly and reassembly. It supports the physical workflow's difficulty, not a claimed number of hours this software could save.
- [buildingSMART Romania consultancy](https://www.buildingsmartromania.org/en/consultanta) offers specific BIM software support at €100/hour, emerging use-case work at €110/hour, and mature use-case work at €120/hour. These are advertised offers for an adjacent skilled workflow, not observed willingness to buy this integration.

## Strong alternatives and prior art

- [IMS installation/removal path analysis](https://fl-ims.com/services/installation-path-interference-analysis) already offers measured 6DOF movement, full-route clearance, alternative routes, temporary obstacle removal, graphics and engineering handoff. It is an exact service comparator. It also explains why current as-built capture matters and why geometry is only part of planning.
- [Fuzor paths documentation](https://fuzor-manual.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/F2UM1/pages/288692112/Paths) supports user-created paths, equipment placement and animation. Automatic priced intervention search must be compared specifically; absence from one documentation page is not proof no product feature exists.
- [ISARC 2016 BIM motion-planning paper](https://www.iaarc.org/publications/fulltext/ISARC2016-Paper008.pdf) already applies OMPL to BIM. This is a known technical transfer. The remaining hypothesis is practical integration and reduced repeated setup/exploration work, not scientific invention.

## Proposed differentiation to test

Reuse IFC element identities and explicit user-authorized reversible-obstacle costs to produce a small set of geometry-checked access alternatives for a contractor's estimate. Measure computational feasibility/coverage and intervention cost against strong simple methods on the same files; leave actual human labor, current-site accuracy, margin and purchase willingness to a bounded consented pilot.

No structural member, live service, safety equipment, or unknown element should become removable just because the optimizer prefers it. An offline output is a geometric hypothesis for qualified review, never permission to move equipment or remove obstacles. Public/synthetic IFC geometry can establish the interface and checks; it cannot establish current-site access or physical safety.
