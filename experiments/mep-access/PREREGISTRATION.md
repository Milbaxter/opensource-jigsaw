# MEP access-options experiment: prospective protocol

Prepared 2026-09-18; **no prototype executed**. Parent publication must precede execution. Rubric v2 remains unchanged. Researcher proposes the experiment and cannot supply its independent final judgment. This tests an offline geometric bid-preparation aid, not rigging, structural, occupational-safety or execution authorization.

## Buyer, combination, falsifiable contribution

Target: a mechanical contractor's preconstruction estimator or its BIM consultant preparing replacement-equipment access options. Proposed deliverable: IFC-linked candidate paths, the explicitly permitted components that each option would temporarily remove, contractor-supplied intervention costs and modeled clearance limits. It feeds the existing engineering workflow.

IfcOpenShell 0.8.5 reads IFC solids/placements/units and component IDs. OMPL 2.0.1 supplies an actual SE(2) probabilistic roadmap for a rigid rectangular unit moving level on an idealized caster platform. Shapely 2.1.2 checks projected geometry; an original set-cost graph search chooses a motion and the union of permitted removals, charging each once. No generic hand-coded path search will be presented as an OMPL result. No trained model or private service is required.

Claim to test: on the same conservatively checked roadmap, jointly choosing route and allowed interventions can produce cheaper declared access options than predeclared route alternatives or a local greedy intervention policy. A separate exhaustive subset oracle checks the best attainable cost **on that roadmap**. The test does not establish global continuous-space optimality, faster human work, lower real project cost, or superiority over IMS/other engineering providers. An engineer may already choose the same best option. Published planning research and existing services support feasibility/category demand; they are not automatic disqualifications or evidence of legal permission.

The local proof is deliberately limited to horizontal translation and yaw at fixed elevation: no tilt, stairs, lifting, deformable equipment, floor capacity, support, stability, rigging, workers or cable/pipe connections. Genuine SE(2) robotics capability suffices for this narrower moving-on-a-level-floor interface. Do not advertise it as full 6DOF analysis.

## Fixed test domain and cases

Generate original synthetic IFC4 files with extruded rectangular solids, named stable component IDs, declared class/removability and positive intervention costs. Retain both generator inputs and actual IFC files/hashes. Planning must consume geometry reloaded from IFC, not bypass parsing by consuming the input JSON. Costs/start/goal may reside in an accompanying case JSON keyed to IFC IDs; geometry may not. Author owns these generated fixtures; they contain no customer data.

Base floor domain: rectangle12m×10m. Unit dimensions1.8m×0.8m×1.4m, reference center at half height. Start(1.5,5,0yaw), goal(10.5,5,0yaw); all dimensions in meters unless unit-transform case specifies millimeters. Minimum modeled clearance50mm plus a numerical tolerance of0.1mm. Walls have thickness0.20m and height3m; ordinary opening width1.4m unless a case says otherwise. A vertical barrier consists of separate forbidden wall solids between openings centered at y=2,5,8. A permitted panel occupying an opening is a removable obstacle, not a structural wall.

1. **Intact/open:** no barrier; one forbidden column centered(6,5), footprint1×1m. Valid detour without interventions expected. Any paid removal recommendation fails this control.
2. **Blocked:** forbidden full-width barrier at x=6 from y=0 to10. Report no found route; the fully spanning separator independently proves disconnection within this planar floor model. Never remove a forbidden element.
3. **Rotation-required:** barrier at x=6 with only the central opening, width1.05m; start/goal yaw90degrees. Valid yaw change is required to traverse. Fixed-yaw90degrees cannot fit; finding no route is a search failure rather than proof no turning path exists.
4. **Clearance-edge:** same case with opening width0.89m. Unit's minimum width0.8m plus both50mm clearance margins exceeds opening width. A reported certified path fails the control. Exact threshold0.90m is conservatively rejected as tolerance-boundary;0.91m is a separate near-boundary search case with uncertainty/censoring reported.
5. **Single barrier options:** x=6, three openings with removable panels costing[1000,700,200] currency units in lower/middle/upper order. A valid intact route is unavailable. This is an easy case on which credible manual alternatives may already find the best option; no advantage is required here.
6. **Two barrier options:** barriers x=4 and8; three openings each. First panel costs[100,300,900], second[900,300,100]. Structural walls and all unclassified objects remain forbidden. This tests coupling between access choices rather than optimizing one obstacle independently.
7. **Operational/overhead:** repeat case6 but classify the first lower panel as operationally forbidden even though its nominal cost is low. Add a transverse beam at x=6 spanning the floor, horizontal thickness0.2m, with bottom elevation1.60m. It clears the1.4m unit plus margin. A separate low-beam variant has bottom1.44m and is an analytic planar-height obstruction; it cannot be removed. Do not project overhead objects into fictitious collisions when vertical intervals provably separate.
8. **Units/placement equivalence:** repeat case6 expressed in millimeters and with nested building/storey placements applying translation(100,200)m and yaw30degrees. Transform floor/start/goal consistently. Extracted world geometry normalized to meters must agree with the transformed meter fixture within0.1mm; resulting optimal intervention cost must match on equivalent transformed roadmap states. This is an invariance check, not a fresh stochastic optimality comparison.

Add 12 prospective perturbed two-barrier fixtures, seeds7300–7311: use Python random.Random(seed); unit length uniform[1.6,2.0], width uniform[0.6,1.0], height1.4; each opening center independently perturbed uniform[-0.15,+0.15]m around2,5,8; independently shuffle costs[100,300,900] for each barrier; choose uniformly one of the six panels to mark operationally forbidden. Retain every case, including inconvenient/infeasible ones. No reweighting or regeneration after observing results. First build and hash all fixtures and implementation before the first geometry/planner execution. The designed controls are explanatory test cases; randomized cases remain synthetic and are not a representative distribution of buildings.

## Geometry normalization and conservative motion checking

Use IfcOpenShell world-coordinate output and its default internal meter convention; record declared IFC unit scale and settings. Do not multiply meter-valued geometry a second time. Reload actual triangulated solids, take conservative convex projected footprints only for obstacles whose vertical interval intersects the unit's vertical interval expanded by50mm. This projection is exact for these rectangular extrusions and may overrestrict general geometry; arbitrary real nonconvex IFC support is not claimed. Preserve IFC IDs in every output.

The ordinary OMPL state validator is insufficient to certify continuous motion. After roadmap extraction, independently validate every used edge under linear center interpolation and shortest-angle yaw interpolation. At the midpoint footprint compute geometric separation d from each obstacle and the floor boundary. For an interval with full translational change T, yaw change a and unit corner radius R, maximum displacement from midpoint is bounded by B=|T|/2+2R*sin(|a|/4). If d>B+clearance+tolerance, the entire interval is separated. Otherwise subdivide; a midpoint collision fails that interval. Stop subdivision at maximum bound1mm or depth20; unresolved intervals are conservatively treated as blocked/uncertain, never free. Retain subdivision counts and numerical margins. Check floor containment using the same bound.

An optional obstacle intersected or unresolved along an otherwise hard-valid edge contributes its ID to that edge's removal set. A hard/operational/unknown obstacle invalidates the edge. For the selected path, rerun the independent verifier with all declared removals applied. Unknown or uncertified edges cannot appear in a reported verified path. Report an explicitly **conservative verification relative to the supplied synthetic solids and planar assumptions**, not an unrestricted route certificate or physical safety guarantee. No finite waypoint-only check can satisfy this requirement.

## Roadmap, optimization, strong baselines

Use genuine OMPL PRM over SE(2), ignoring only explicitly permitted removable obstacles while respecting fixed obstacles and floor bounds in state validity. Fixed OMPL seeds7400,7401,7402 per ordinary fixture, in fresh processes to isolate random state. Build each roadmap with a2-second planning budget, retain PlannerData vertices/edges and termination status, then filter/label its edges conservatively. Store exact graphs because wall-clock termination may yield different graphs on another host. A rerun on the stored graph is deterministic; a regenerated graph need not be identical. Preserve disconnected/time-limited outcomes. If bindings/API cannot support extraction or seeded execution, halt and document; no silent replacement planner.

Optimize lexicographically: first total permitted-removal cost, then path length using translation plus R×absolute yaw change. Set-cost search state includes roadmap vertex and the union of removed IDs, with exact positive costs; a component is paid once across all edges. No cost is assigned to forbidden removal. Search-space cap100,000 expanded states per roadmap,60seconds; report unknown on cap exhaustion. Synthetic prices are scenario inputs, not sourced contractor quotes or estimated project savings.

For the **same geometry/graph**, compare:

- **Manually specified candidate-route proxy:** straight route plus lower, middle and upper corridor routes. For two barriers each route goes through the respective same-index opening centers on both barriers, using staging points1.2m before/after barriers; it can turn to yaw0 in open staging space and restore the goal yaw. Check complete motions and union of removal costs with the same verifier. Publish the exact waypoint construction. This is a fixed plausible candidate set, **not a measurement of what a competent engineer would choose**; a human could specify additional/better routes.
- **Greedy intervention baseline:** on the full conservative roadmap, find a route minimizing the sum of the number of remaining optional labels per edge, breaking ties by geometric length. Remove the cheapest still-blocking permitted component on that route and repeat until the selected route needs no further removal or no route exists. Charge the union of all removed components, including wasted decisions. Also report the cost-aware variant that replaces label count with the sum of remaining per-edge costs, acknowledging repeated-cost double counting. Compare against the better of these two greedy baselines.
- **Exhaustive small-case oracle:** enumerate every permitted removal subset (at most6optional elements), discard edges whose label sets are not contained in the subset, and compute shortest paths. The smallest feasible subset cost is an exact oracle for this finite conservatively checked roadmap. Compare the joint solver against it on every ordinary/randomized case, not just successful examples.
- **No-intervention planning:** same roadmap with no allowed removals, recording whether an intact route is available. Never claim an intervention benefit where a valid zero-cost path already exists.

Required controls: two obvious separated/colliding poses; rotation-sensitive passage; between-waypoint collision; a tangency/boundary case; unit/placement invariance; forbidden low-cost temptation; overhead clearance; all positive/known costs; missing geometry or unknown unit rejection. Missing geometry is an input failure, never empty free space.

## Prospective outcome and evidence rules

A confirmed bridge requires IFC-produced geometry to feed genuine OMPL states/edges, optimized ID sets to map back to IFC objects, independent continuous conservative checks to accept every reported path, and exact agreement with exhaustive finite-roadmap cost on every completed search. Wrong forbidden removal, unit handling, or false-free continuous edge is a critical failure. Retain failed runs before correcting implementation and refreeze corrected code; do not silently exclude them.

A **demonstrated proxy advantage** additionally requires lower intervention cost than the better greedy baseline and the candidate-route proxy in at least 3 of the 12 randomized fixtures, with median relative improvement of at least 10% on those qualifying fixtures and no invalid reported paths. This is a prospective technical screen, not a replacement for v2 or evidence of real buyer savings. If no such advantage is found, report that the bridge works but its proposed improvement was not demonstrated. Runtime/setup/graph sizes are descriptive; no claimed human labor saving without timing actual human work. Record per-case/seed outcomes, graph and file hashes, times, selected paths/IDs/costs, oracle comparisons, and all excluded/error classifications. A successful narrow result still needs independent commercial judging.

Local prototype cap:12 engineer-hours and€50 compute, without paid services. No engine execution before published protocol. Dependencies and exact wheel hashes are in package-pins.json; artifact downloads must pass SHA-256 and ZIP magic checks.
