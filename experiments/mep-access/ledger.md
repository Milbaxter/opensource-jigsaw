# Round 3C: buyer-first physical workflow screen

Astra discovery, 2026-09-18. Eight fresh hypotheses screened before implementation. No scored pass, prototype, outreach, or claim that published algorithms make every business opportunity impossible. The rejection criterion here is narrower: the proposed combination has no evidenced useful advantage over a practical named alternative. Component names below are proposed combinations; exact version/license diligence was not completed after an early substantive rejection.

| ID | Buyer / costly workflow | Proposed OSS transfer | Strongest evidence and counterevidence | Disposition |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| P01 | Commercial heat treaters batch orders across furnaces while meeting due dates and managing energy/setup costs | Cantera or CoolProp process model + OR-Tools scheduling + tariff data | A manufacturing case study already integrated dynamic heat-treatment scheduling with ERP, including setups, rush orders and breakdowns. FurnXpert sells thermal furnace analysis. A generic physics-plus-scheduler would need a narrower measured gap than tariff-aware batching. [Primary paper](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0278612517301619), [FurnXpert pricing](https://www.furnxpert.com/purchase) | Do not prototype the generic concept. |
| P02 | Battery reuse technicians match heterogeneous retired cells into application-constrained packs | PyBaMM electrochemistry + CVXPY/HiGHS robust assignment + uncertainty estimates | A July2026 primary paper already jointly optimizes cell matching, inverter/energy feasibility, resistance/capacity/self-discharge mismatch and screening uncertainty; its comparator is precisely single-metric sorting. Public-data replay would reproduce its concept, while physical safety/economic outcomes require separate validation. [Primary paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.12951) | Reject current mechanism; no defensible new advantage identified. |
| P03 | Soil-carbon project developers pay for field travel and lab samples | GeoPandas/Rasterio + uncertainty-driven stratification + OR-Tools routes | The2019 ospats+ primary paper already maximizes farmer profit from sampling design and uncertainty. GXLab and Smart Cloud Farming offer sampling/stratification; Pique advertises GPS route optimization. Adding routing to a sampling model is insufficient without a concrete joint-objective advantage. [Primary paper](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0308521X18310801), [GXLab](https://gxlab.com/products/software/), [Smart Cloud Farming](https://smartcloudfarming.com/products/sampling-design), [Pique](https://www.piqueplatform.com/) | Reject broad proposition; no claim all joint-design opportunities exhausted. |
| P04 | Ground-source heat-pump designers pay for long on-site thermal response tests | pygfunction ground response + PyMC sequential uncertainty + optimal stopping | A2015 primary paper already derives test stopping from statistical stability while explicitly discussing cost versus duration. Merely showing earlier convergence on public/synthetic traces would not establish valid shorter field tests or distinct buyer value. [Primary paper](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s40517-015-0025-5) | Reject generic adaptive stopping; field-specific validation needed for a narrower proposition. |
| P05 | Process-plant shutdown planners trace isolations across drawings and coordinate outages | pyDEXPI + NetworkX graph cuts + OR-Tools work bundling | PlantQuest already simulates isolation completeness and shutdown impacts; KonnectxD explicitly derives isolation boundaries/testpacks from DEXPI graphs; Innovapptive groups jobs around shared downtime. iPlanSTO has a public procurement offer £75k–£300k/year, establishing a paid category, not a gap. [PlantQuest](https://www.plantquest.com/product/simulator), [KonnectxD](https://konnectxd.com/apps/smart-pid), [Innovapptive](https://www.innovapptive.com/software/planning-and-scheduling-software), [Procurement listing](https://www.applytosupply.digitalmarketplace.service.gov.uk/g-cloud/services/947307610928330), [pyDEXPI](https://www.pi-research.org/software/pydexpi/) | Reject broad bundle. Safety and site-specific topology prevent treating a graph-only proof as an operational isolation plan. |
| P06 | Mechanical contractors price large equipment replacement/removal through constrained buildings | IfcOpenShell + OMPL + minimum-cost obstacle-removal search | Parent located explicit paid installation-path services and2016 BIM/OMPL work. Stronger inverse wedge would minimize temporary demolition/removal rather than find a path, but minimum-constraint-removal is established robotics; a newly found patent text also describes BIM equipment routes with priced removable obstacles. That patent text remains third-party/unverified because direct GooglePatents access failed; it is a lead for further review, not a verified freedom-to-operate conclusion. [Paid service](https://fl-ims.com/services/installation-path-interference-analysis), [BIM/OMPL paper](https://www.iaarc.org/publications/fulltext/ISARC2016-Paper008.pdf), [Primary MCR paper](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/view/12100), [Unverified patent lead](https://eureka.patsnap.com/patent/CN122288659A) | Hold at research, no prototype. A specific lower-effort engineering deliverable could still be valuable, but none evidenced yet. |
| P07 | Foundries spend on scrap/alloy charges and corrections when chemistry varies | pycalphad thermodynamics + HiGHS blend optimization + probabilistic feedstock chemistry | OptiCharge explicitly handles uncertain scrap chemistry, safety buffers, cost, metallurgical constraints and what-if baskets. SavingCAST includes mechanical-property analysis; ALEA publishes a named buyer's payback statement. A generic robust-cheaper blend merely repeats existing functionality. Nonlinear phase-aware constraints might be a deeper wedge but essential thermodynamic database rights and plant validation remain unverified. [OptiCharge](https://www.mpmindia.com/foundry/opticharge/), [SavingCAST](https://www.c3p-group.com/savingcast/savingcast.html), [ALEA buyer statement](https://aleasoftware.com/en/services/charge-calculation/) | Reject generic robust blend; do not inflate unknown nonlinear extension into a ready lead. |
| P08 | CNC shops maintain coolant concentration/pH to avoid damaged tools, parts and wasted fluid | FilterPy state estimation + physical mass balances + constrained replenishment optimization | Okuma directly describes the costly failure and sells a monitoring option. Zebra's Dazzle already continuously measures/mixes fluid across multiple sumps and supports manual-reading workflows. Generic sensor fusion plus dosing adds no evidenced advantage; a cheap offline model still requires process-specific measurements to demonstrate benefit. [Okuma](https://www.okuma.com/coolant-monitoring-system), [Zebra](https://coolantautomation.zebraskimmers.com/dazzle-2) | Reject broad proposition. |

The cheapest next step for any surviving *commercial* angle would be scoped practitioner discovery, but no outbound communication is authorized here. No prospect has agreed to provide data or buy anything. This screen conserves prototype time by finding exact competitors early; it does not establish that no viable physical-workflow business exists.
