# BAS mapping: independent commercial, prior-art and access review

2026-09-18. Independent Astra reviewer B. No candidate predictions or benchmark results were available when this note was written. It does not set or amend experimental thresholds and makes no pursuit recommendation. The proposed scope is an offline, human-reviewed BAS import/mapping package using ontology constraints, time-series evidence and constrained assignment.

## Paid work is real; the available quote is historical

Virginia Tech's official [VTS-913-2018 contract packet](https://www.procurement.vt.edu/content/dam/procurement_vt_edu/contracts/documents/VTS-913-2018.pdf) contains a signed services contract and attached pricing. Its pricing presentation, PDF page 124 / printed slide 16, allocates 25.8 hours and $3,900 to point mapping, including virtual points, and 8.6 hours / $1,300 to preparing a bulk-asset configuration sheet. The latter was already expected to be automated. These are components of a 172-hour / $26,000 initial 500-point building example, not an addressable saving of $26,000. Graphics, asset classes and fault rules are separate work. Earlier bulk-pricing assumptions concern a different average point count and commitment; do not combine them into one rate.

The original contract dates to June 2018; the latest signed renewal in the inspected packet ends June 11, 2025. This is stronger evidence than a speculative market-size estimate, but it does not establish a current 2026 rate, invoiced hours, full payment, or repeat demand for this entrant. The local 19,229,375-byte file has actual PDF magic and SHA-256 `1fd152440c9735adeb0ebfd4bbcfd753befa57aaec04140caf42346984290b7d`.

A plausible narrow buyer is the controls integration project manager or engineering lead responsible for onboarding a building portfolio, with a university/facility energy team as the accepting customer. Mapping may be a fixed-price integrator cost that a tool reduces, or billable labor the supplier has less incentive to eliminate. That incentive must be learned rather than assumed.

## Strong alternatives already combine these ideas

| Alternative | Inspected primary capability | Implication |
|---|---|---|
| [Clockworks ontology mapping](https://clockworksanalytics.com/ontologies-ai-building-metadata-mapping/) | April 2025 article describes using ontology mappings to restrict learned point-type predictions when tags are incomplete or ambiguous. | Ontology plus constrained learned inference is already commercial prior art. Different algorithms alone do not establish a useful wedge. |
| [Clockworks current onboarding](https://clockworksanalytics.com/building-analytics-frequently-asked-questions/) | Name-based point/equipment inference, automatic document extraction, equipment configuration inference, model validation; subscription scoped to connected equipment. | A name-only classifier is a limited proxy for the full incumbent workflow. The incumbent already has document/context signals missing from public time-series benchmarks. |
| [Clockworks March 2026 release](https://clockworksanalytics.com/onboarding-design-updates/) | Onboarding QA highlights missing, flat and out-of-range data; validated workbook bulk import and deployment tracking. | Reviewed import and quality checks are not inherently new product features. |
| [Mapped pricing](https://www.mapped.com/pricing) | Paid discovery, extraction, normalization and retention; current page displays a $250/month gateway option. Free starter excludes BAS/BMS/HVAC sources. | A paid normalization category exists; the gateway price is neither the entire platform price nor a mapping-service price. Do not incorrectly call the free sandbox a free production BAS substitute. |
| [Plaster](https://github.com/plastering/plastering) | OSS framework unifies naming and time-series workflows, active labeling, and confidence-tagged Brick output. | A new composition must show a practical improvement over established metadata-normalization approaches. Old code or inaccessible original training data do not erase prior art. |
| [Unified Architecture, 2020](https://arxiv.org/abs/2003.07690) | Authors combine point names and time series using supervised classification, unsupervised clustering, deterministic/probabilistic outputs and deployment processing. | Cross-modal classification for BAS is established research; reported accuracy is from its own datasets and cannot be compared numerically with a new benchmark. |
| [Brick-DICL, June 2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.17637v1) | Primary preprint uses retrieved metadata/classes and model disagreement to flag low-confidence cases for expert review. | Reducing manual verification by abstaining on ambiguous classifications is also prior art. This is a research description, not evidence of a deployed paid product or an independently reproduced accuracy claim. |
| [Brick formal semantics](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/built-environment/articles/10.3389/fbuil.2020.558034/full) | Brick+ formalizes tags, inference and SHACL constraints. | SHACL validity establishes compliance with encoded constraints; it does not prove a point's physical identity or correct downstream operational behavior. |

A possible service wedge remains: a portable offline deliverable for integrators who cannot or will not buy a full analytics/data-layer platform, with demonstrably less review effort on their actual target import schema. That is an untested inference, not a discovered market gap. Vendor neutrality, local execution and expert sign-off may matter; none alone proves durable value. Existing integrators can copy rules and classifiers, and incumbent mapping systems already benefit from deployed portfolios.

## Public data can support a real bounded comparison

The official [BTS repository](https://github.com/cruiseresearchgroup/DIEF_BTS) now points to the full raw dataset, not only the old training partition. Independent Figshare API inspection confirms [raw v3](https://figshare.com/articles/dataset/BTS_Building_Timeseries_Dataset_Raw/28705559), DOI `10.6084/m9.figshare.28705559.v3`, published April 3, 2025, with a **CC BY 4.0** license. The former train-only record is DOI `10.6084/m9.figshare.25912180.v1`, also CC BY 4.0. API records, file sizes and available upstream hashes are preserved locally. No full time-series archive was downloaded by this reviewer.

The raw record offers three site metadata CSVs, three Brick graphs, Brick 1.2.1 definitions and large site time-series archives. Site B alone is about 1.51 GB, while A and C are about 8.48 and 8.98 GB. The repository notes four intentionally missing Site A files, and not every stream has a file. Metadata includes labels and statistics: these are evaluation/training assets, not automatically permissible model inputs in a held-out comparison.

The [official data card](https://github.com/cruiseresearchgroup/DIEF_BTS/blob/main/BTS_DataCards.md) identifies three Australian buildings, UUID anonymization, uncleaned telemetry and expert-built DCH/Brick models; it records annotations as not independently validated. It cautions against direct production generalization. Its old embargo text is superseded by the current raw release. No private customer data is needed for a public offline demonstration, but public labels do not certify a new site's operational mapping or demonstrate real review-time savings.

The repository's code license is MIT; data rights are separately CC BY 4.0, so attribution, license link and changes must follow the data when redistributed. The latest repository metadata and actual license file were fetched and hashed. Brick repository metadata declares BSD-3-Clause; exact runtime package/version rights still require inspection after the implementation is pinned. No blanket exact-version clearance is implied for an as-yet unselected solver or SHACL library.

## Recruitment and real customer input access

There is a concrete population beyond “sell to enterprises.” [Clockworks' partner program](https://clockworksanalytics.com/partners/) identifies mechanical/controls service providers and named examples including Gardiner, Wendel and LONG. [SkyFoundry's channel](https://skyfoundry.com/partners/) lists resellers, distributors and OEMs; [its company page](https://skyfoundry.com/company) describes system integrators and specialist engineering firms. These identify relevant firms and public business inquiry routes, not relationships or willing participants. Their existing platforms are also competitors.

A realistic validation transaction is an authorized offline export from a controls integrator: point inventory, agreed time-series slice, relevant units/equipment context and the customer's target import template, followed by an engineer's reviewed mapping. Existing [Clockworks API documentation](https://cw.clockworksanalytics.com/APIDocumentation.aspx) illustrates that point descriptions, types, datasource IDs, units and raw time series are actual structured platform objects. It does not grant access to any customer's data or prove every customer can export the same fields. Drawings, occupied-space metadata and proprietary naming dictionaries may have separate contractual restrictions.

Public BTS access therefore clears a local research input barrier but does not replace consented customer access. A commercial experiment could reasonably test acquisition of such exports without live controller credentials, on-site commissioning, autonomous writes, or importing unreviewed point assignments. Access denial, unavailable truth, incompatible target import formats and excessive expert correction are material failure outcomes. Customer-derived mappings cannot become a cross-client corpus unless reuse rights and privacy constraints permit it.

## Open questions for later independent judgment

No scores are assigned before the technical evidence. The key unresolved claims are incremental review effort at a controlled error rate, robustness to actual new-site metadata and equipment variation, downstream import acceptance, total onboarding/support cost, separate willingness to pay, and a credible source of continuing value beyond public code assembly. A public classifier result may establish a useful mechanism while leaving the commercial proposition below the frozen pursuit threshold. Conversely, the existence of prior art alone does not invalidate a demonstrated practical advantage.
