# MEP access options: independent context before results

Independent Astra reviewer B, 2026-09-18. Read the parent-published protocol commit `4464c4d`, `PARENT-EVIDENCE.md`, and the proposer's practical-wedge note. No prototype results were reviewed before writing this note. This is context for a later v2 judgment, not a changed protocol, a verdict, or an operational approval.

## Commercial task and bounded opportunity

A mechanical contractor's preconstruction estimator or BIM consultant must establish how replacement equipment can reach its destination before quoting equipment, temporary works and installation. [Moore's actual chiller project](https://www.mooreusa.com/projects/empr-chilled-water-plant/) describes an oversized chiller, constrained openings, a planned wall opening and custom positioning skids. [PSC's replacement](https://pscind.com/projects/chiller-removal-and-replacement) describes a restricted access route and equipment disassembly. These contractor accounts establish the physical task; full-project contract value and installation man-hours are not planning labor or software savings.

A direct commercial category exists: [IMS installation-path analysis](https://fl-ims.com/services/installation-path-interference-analysis) supplies measured geometry, complete movement checks, alternate routes, temporary-obstacle analysis and graphical/dimensional deliverables. The page explicitly reserves rigging and engineering approval to qualified customer teams. Its service is the strongest practical comparator found, not a reason by itself to reject a more efficient or differently packaged tool.

A current offered skilled-work rate is available from [buildingSMART Romania](https://www.buildingsmartromania.org/en/consultanta): €100/hour for application-specific support, €110/hour for emerging uses, and €120/hour for established BIM workflows. It includes IFC export, coordination and applied integration. This is adjacent consultancy pricing, not an observed access-analysis invoice or evidence that the proposed €500 packet has a buyer.

The proposed practical product is narrower than replacement of engineering services: an offline packet linking candidate motions and the union of permitted interventions back to IFC IDs and a contractor cost worksheet. Reusing geometry and producing several priced options could save setup/exploration work. That is a plausible hypothesis even though motion planning and removal-set optimization are established methods. The value must survive input preparation, clearance review and correction effort.

A viable integration might win through reliable import/units/placements, conservative checks, consistent IFC IDs, input rejection and a useful output in an existing workflow. Those features can matter even if every algorithm is known. Their continuing value must still be concrete: maintainable adapters, verified project templates and a trustworthy review process are possibilities, not an acquired corpus or demonstrated moat. An implementation that only reconstructs a standard optimizer with weak comparisons should not receive extra novelty credit for component count.

## Real inputs are procurable, not presently owned

[C&S](https://www.cscos.com/projects/chiller-replacement-and-plant-upgrades/) describes scanning a chiller facility for replacement/upgrades. [MaRS's MEP service](https://scantobimsolutions.com/services/mep-bim-modeling.html) offers Revit/IFC-associated MEP modeling, access zones, supports/hangers and a removal-route clearance study that may affect equipment selection. This is a credible contractor-to-scan/BIM-provider path for obtaining a commissioned extract. It is stronger access evidence than assuming old design drawings are current.

These sources do not promise a ready-to-use IFC file with every needed support, clearance or removal permission. The contractor/customer must authorize use, confirm scope and supply costs and removable classifications. Unknown items stay forbidden. An IFC class or visually removable panel cannot supply engineering permission; the prototype correctly treats permission as external input. Existing scans/models may require corrections or represent proprietary facilities. Those costs and permissions remain first-stage pilot checks.

Named provider firms and public business inquiry routes create an accessible recruitment population. No relationship, response, customer export, model purchase or outreach is implied. Proposed public synthetic examples can support initial discussions, but cannot stand in for the two permissioned extracts required for the later paired analyst study.

## What the prospective benchmark can resolve

The protocol tests a genuine integration if geometry is reloaded from authored IFC, consumed by actual OMPL SE(2) roadmap generation, independently filtered by continuous conservative checks, and linked back to allowed IFC removals with costs counted once. Millimeter/nested-placement equivalence and overhead handling are material integration checks, not decorative dependencies.

The claim is deliberately finite and planar: rigid translation/yaw at fixed elevation on an idealized level caster interface. Minimum clearance is relative to supplied synthetic solids. A valid result is neither 6DOF analysis nor evidence of safe rigging, floor capacity, support, stability or actual installation feasibility. That boundary permits an offline research comparison and human-reviewed options study; it does not authorize operational movement.

The fixed candidate-route set is a disclosed manual-route proxy, not a measured engineer baseline. Cost-aware and count-based greedy variants provide more credible automatic comparisons. The exhaustive subset method is an exact simple alternative on these tiny graphs (at most six optional elements). Agreement checks the joint solver; no claim of superiority over enumeration should be made without an actual measured reason. Stored graph replay separates deterministic optimization from wall-clock-limited roadmap generation.

The frozen success rule requires a cost improvement over both the stronger greedy variant and candidate-route proxy on at least three of twelve prospective synthetic perturbations, with the declared magnitude and no invalid paths. A successful result would establish that narrow cost-choice advantage only. Synthetic cost savings cannot be translated into project ROI, and sparse roadmaps or censored search cannot establish continuous-space infeasibility except for separate analytic controls.

## Commercial hypotheses retained for the pilot

The proposed three-week study caps total effort/cash at €3,000: 24 fully loaded hours at €100/hour plus €600 model preparation/compute. It seeks two authorized extracts and counterbalanced analyst comparisons, targets 30% less preparation time with correctness review and one paid repeat packet of at least €500, and kills on unavailable/expensive inputs, equal incumbent effort, correctness failure or no repeat purchase.

That is an experiment definition, not observed unit economics. For a €500 repeat packet, approximately five analyst hours at the cited €100/hour rate consume all revenue before sales, preparation, review, software upkeep or margin; actual delivery effort must be lower or value/price higher. The €3,000 research cap is reasonable as a cap but does not validate a profitable steady-state service. Whether enough relevant projects recur per buyer, whether a contractor can charge the packet to bids, whether existing consultants already match the workflow effort, and whether outputs avoid costly rework remain open. A short trial can test acquisition, task effort and repeat purchase; it cannot demonstrate annual retention.

No score is assigned before frozen results and independent technical verification. Known algorithms and established suppliers are counterevidence to broad novelty claims, not automatic rejection criteria. Likewise, a working bridge or a favorable synthetic example is not an automatic pursuit pass.
