# Machinery follow-up: primary context

Observed18September2026, before any candidate performance evaluation. These are source observations and hypotheses, not a scored pursuit.

- [Tetra Pak Asset Health Monitoring](https://www.tetrapak.com/campaigns/maintenance-services/asset-health-monitoring-services) offers sensor kits, analytics, expert interpretation and notifications as an existing maintenance service. This establishes an adjacent commercial category and strong supplier channel, not a buyer willing to purchase our proposed integration.
- [Tetra Pak separator customer case](https://www.tetrapak.com/content/dam/tetrapak/media-box/global/en/services/maintenance-service-packaging/predictive-maintenance-packaging/documents/Case%20Predictive%20Maintenance%20Seperators.pdf) describes a Spanish dairy's service contract and supplier-estimated€45,000 avoided costs from bearing monitoring. This is a separator, not an independent cart system; the estimated avoided event is not a measured counterfactual or candidate revenue. No quoted price or entrant economics follows.
- [Jabbar et al., ISMA2024 proceedings](https://past.isma-isaac.be/downloads/isma2024/proceedings/Contribution_573_proceeding_3.pdf) describes stationary sensors receiving nearby-cart vibration and other-cart interference, and distinguishes fault detection from identifying the faulty bearing/cart. This directly supports the technical attribution problem. It does not establish an unresolved advantage for an audio-source-separation implementation.
- [Adaptive distribution transformation, ISA Transactions2026](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.isatra.2026.06.005) already uses MOIRA-UNIMORE signals to address nonstationary independent-cart bearing diagnosis without a deep model. [Related IEEE research](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/abstract/document/11264538/) explicitly identifies interacting-bearing source attribution. Any proposed test needs these alternatives considered alongside simple position-conditioned passage gating.

The business hypothesis is a reviewed maintenance attribution report from already-authorized machine recordings. No machine control, inspection approval, contact, purchase or deployment has occurred.
