** Data summary for: Data for Advanced Metrology Suite for Linking Residual  **
** Stress to Fundamental Properties of Thermoset Packaging Materials         **
*******************************************************************************

Authors: Polette Centellas, Stian Romberg, Ran Tao, Alexander K Landauer

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DATA SOURCES (top level folders):
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DIC_data: Data for the digital image correlation (DIC) experiment, including 
          example raw data (images), setup information and metadata in their 
		  native format and as .csv or .txt files, and processed results (.mat). 
		  The figure is included as a .png.

DSC_data: Reduced data in .xlsx files from DSC experiments used to generate 
          figures for the associated ECTC manuscript and the resultant figures 
		  in .qpc and .tif formats.
                    
Rheology_data: Reduced data from a parallel plate separate actuator-transducer 
               rheometer in the form of .csv files used to generate figures.

Stress_bench_data: Data from a stress bench setup equipped with a multi-beam 
                   optical stress sensor that monitors changes in the thin film 
				   curvature during thermal cycling experiments, similar 
				   measurements where the curvature is measured during the 
				   curing process, and water vapor sorption-desorption 
				   experiments. Reduced data are included in .csv and .xlsx 
				   files, with the resultant figures as .png images.
