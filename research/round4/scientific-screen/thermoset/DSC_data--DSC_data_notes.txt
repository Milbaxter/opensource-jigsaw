
Notes on the differential scanning calorimetry (DSC) measurements:

All measurements are performed by Ran Tao using a TA Instruments Discovery 
DSC 2500 equipped with a refrigerated cooling system under nitrogen. About 
10 mg to 15 mg of uncured encapsulant samples are sealed in 40 µL hermetic 
pans for measurement.

The standard uncertainties for temperature and heat flow are approximately 
± 1 % and ± 3 %, respectively.

Model-based kinetics analysis and simulation are performed using NETZSCH 
Kinetics Neo software.
