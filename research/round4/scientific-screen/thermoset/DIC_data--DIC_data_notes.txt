
Notes on the stereo digital image correlation (DIC) measurements:

All measurements are performed by Alexander Landauer using a pair of 24.4 MPx 
cameras (ace2 a2A5328-15umPRO Monochrome, Basler AG) with 50 mm lenses (Fujinon 
CF50ZA-1S, Fujifilm Corp.) in a custom environmental chamber (based on a GF-C2 
oven from DDM Novastar, Ivyland, PA) with a large antireflective coated
window (approximately 300 mm square) and 3 LED light panels for illumination.
A roughly circular epoxy blob (approximately 600 mg to 800 mg) was allowed to 
self-level on a flexible backer before being speckled with white spray paint.

The customary DIC parameter table is as follows:

|-----------------------------------------------------------|
|    Parameter [unit]             Value                     |
|-----------------------------------------------------------|
 Region of Interest (ROI) [px]    Approx. 910 × 895    
 Aperture                         f/16    
 Image scale [µm/px]              1.23    
 Stereo angle [°]                 -18.2    
 Stand-off distance [mm]          241 mm    
 Exposure time [ms]               19    
 Image filtering                  Gaussian 5 px kernel    
 Reference image                  Cumulative with update 
                                  for iteration > 6    
 Interpolation                    Bi-cubic spline    
 Matching criterion               Zero-normalized sum-of-
                                  square differences with 
								  Gaussian weighting    
 Subset size [px]                 41    
 Step size [px]                   19    
 Subset shape function            Affine    
 Strain Window                    21
 Virtual strain gauge [px]        421    
 Strain formulation               Green-Lagrange with Q4 
                                  interpolant    
 Static noise floor [µm/m]        22.4    
|-----------------------------------------------------------|

Stereo-DIC analysis was conducting using MatchID-Stereo in accordance with 
the International Digital Image Correlation Society’s Good Practices Guide.
