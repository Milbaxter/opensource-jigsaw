# Scientific and specialist workflows: follow-up discovery

Research date: 2026-09-18. Pain-first search, then OSS transfer. These ten candidates are additions to the original sixteen. Early kills are not fully licensed or feasibility-audited; component names are proposed combinations, not claims of tested integrations. No business pursuit pass awarded.

| ID | Buyer and workflow | Proposed 2–4-component bridge | Decision and direct evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| S01 | Metabolomics core manager allocating samples to plates/run order | OpenMS + OR-Tools + statsmodels: multivariate balance with carryover constraints | Reject generic optimizer. [Salk paid LC-MS rates](https://www.salk.edu/science/core-facilities/mass-spectrometry-core/scheduling-and-rates/) establish expense, but [Well Plate Maker](https://academic.oup.com/bioinformatics/article/37/17/2770/6128508), [Omixer](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10262301/), [InjectionDesign](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10734102/) and [easyplater](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.17988) already cover constrained and multivariate layouts. |
| S02 | LC-MS service facility rescuing failed injections | OpenMS + OR-Tools + Prefect: adaptive reinjection queue minimizing lost instrument time | Reject. [Agilent LC/MS Workflow Intelligence](https://www.agilent.com/en/product/liquid-chromatography-mass-spectrometry-lc-ms/workflow-intelligence) detects carryover and adds blanks/reinjections; [Waters QCMonitor](https://www.waters.com/nextgen/us/en/products/informatics-and-software/mass-spectrometry-software/masslynx-mass-spectrometry-software/masslynx-quantitation-applications.html) already offers reinject/skip/stop. Hardware integration would increase difficulty while eliminating the proposed novelty. |
| S03 | Assay core deciding bridge samples after instrument/batch disruption | statsmodels + OR-Tools + OpenMS: optimize rerun set for statistical power/cost | Reject generic bridge optimization. [pwrBRIDGE](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9550194/) addresses rerun cost versus power/FDR. [Olink bridging](https://www.icesi.edu.co/CRAN/web/packages/OlinkAnalyze/vignettes/bridging_introduction.html) supplies an existing professional workflow. No distinct mechanism established. |
| S04 | Battery R&D electrochemist choosing next EIS frequencies | impedance.py + PyMC + OR-Tools: discriminate competing equivalent circuits with minimal new measurements | Reject for current lane. [NIST AutoEIS](https://www.nist.gov/publications/autoeis-automated-bayesian-model-selection-and-analysis-electrochemical-impedance) already automates Bayesian circuit selection; [frequency selection research](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.06112) already minimizes measurement load. Additional scientific validation required and a narrow paid wedge not established. |
| S05 | Hydrogeology consultancy planning pumping-test sampling | FloPy + PyMC + OR-Tools: uncertainty-sensitive next observation/time planning | Reject pending stronger evidence. [Parameter nonuniqueness research](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s13201-024-02134-1) supports pain; [AQTESOLV automatic curve matching](https://www.aqtesolv.com/automatic_curve_matching.htm) provides weighted fitting and sophisticated existing analysis. No evidence that the specific proposed capability earns a separate budget or can be validated immediately from one public dataset. |
| S06 | Engineering consultancy checking spreadsheet dimensions | Pint + formulas + Z3: infer units from headers and produce counterexample explanations | Reject. [UCheck](https://web.engr.oregonstate.edu/~erwig/units/) already infers spreadsheet units; [Excel_ent](https://keronengineering.com/software/) already sells dimensional checking. Packaging these pieces is not a defensible new mechanism. |
| S07 | Drone surveyor choosing field-accessible GCP placement | OpenDroneMap + pyproj + OR-Tools: joint geometry and walking-route optimization | Reject current formulation. [Propeller](https://www.propelleraero.com/blog/how-to-optimize-your-ground-control-point-placement-for-drone-surveying/) documents costly GCP geometry mistakes, but [GCP optimization research](https://www.mdpi.com/2071-1050/14/15/9505) already attacks inaccessible placement. Accuracy depends on imagery/terrain and cannot be certified from geometric proxies alone. |
| S08 | Microscopy core tracking instrument QC and provenance | OMERO + MetroloJ_QC + DataLad: reproducible QC history connected to experiments | Reject as familiar integration. [MetroloJ_QC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9526251/) covers detector/optical/stage quality, while [community metadata standards](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41592-021-01327-9) already articulate provenance requirements. No distinctive customer bottleneck beyond integration. |
| S09 | Research lab making editable reproducible publication figures | Matplotlib + svgutils + Pint: preserve units and rerun figure layouts | Reject. [Researcher workflow repository](https://github.com/vdplasthijs/reproducible_figures) illustrates manual work; [Pylustrator](https://arxiv.org/abs/1910.00279) already makes publication figure editing reproducible. Weak payment and differentiation evidence. |
| S10 | Research high-content screening core/CRO analyst allocating segmentation-review time | scikit-image + SciPy + NumPy; prospective napari UI: propagate segmentation sensitivity through assay decisions, rank expected errors corrected | Only prototype candidate. [Primary workflow paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8450896/) links missed rare phenotypes to downstream mistakes and laborious review. [Karolinska fees](https://ki.se/en/research/research-infrastructure-and-environments/core-facilities-for-research/the-luminous-cell-imaging-core-facility/fees) establish paid expertise. Closest competitors include [deepflash2](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-023-36960-9), [coSMicQC](https://github.com/cytomining/coSMicQC), and [SPACe](https://github.com/dlabate/SPACe). Needs decision-specific advantage over uncertainty ranking, not generic QC. Real-image preregistration in assay-qc/PROTOCOL.md. No commercial defensibility or paid conversion yet established. |

The search changed direction after direct competitors eliminated LC-MS planning and EIS model selection. S10 was retained because a narrow decision-aware review allocation mechanism is separable from the generic active-learning/QC products. Retention does not imply novelty over all decision-focused active-learning literature. A held-out failure must remain visible.
