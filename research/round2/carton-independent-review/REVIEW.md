# C07 independent review: watch, 76.5/100

Independent Astra reviewer B, September 18, 2026. I did not propose C07 or implement its search. I inspected the frozen protocol, original implementation and results, checked commercial/prior-art/rights sources, directly reran the five witnesses, and implemented a second interval-based geometry checker without importing the prototype.

The frozen v2 gate does not pass. Scores are novelty 7, buyer pain 8, willingness to pay 8, feasibility 9, defensibility 5, distribution 7, and evidence 9. The weighted result is 76.5/100; defensibility misses its minimum of 6. Confidence that the tightly bounded experiment would be informative is 0.76, not a commercial-success probability. Formal recommendation remains **watch**. All technical/scope gates pass with the limitations below; lack of existing paying customers is not treated as a blocking requirement.

## What the result establishes

Five synthetic transitions across three carton families are genuine py3dbp 1.1.2 counterexamples: shrinking an item causes both supported sorting modes to miss the cheaper carton, despite an independently valid inherited placement. Fresh direct-engine runs and my second geometry implementation confirm all five. The original 48 controls and parent replay also pass. This is useful assurance evidence for an old public heuristic engine, not evidence that current production engines have the same incidence or that physical products can safely be packed this way.

Hypothesis found three fixtures; standard-library random search found two and was faster in this finite run. The distributions differ, so neither discovery-rate superiority nor a meaningful runtime comparison is established. All three Hypothesis shrink runs reached their cap. Dimension totals declined, item counts did not, and serialization shrank by 1–3 bytes. The results do not justify a minimality claim or a Hypothesis competitive advantage over a simple metamorphic harness.

I independently inspected the official [FedEx guide](https://www.fedex.com/content/dam/fedex/us-united-states/services/Service_Guide_2026.pdf): page 127 supports the scoped dimensional thresholds and Zone 2 $29.50 charge; page 135 supports divisor 139 and upward inch rounding. This is a **fee component**, not invoice savings. The failed 1,770-byte direct fetch was HTML. Its checksum must never be described as a PDF hash. The corrected provenance and parent tariff check disclose this protocol deviation; the independent primary-text check supports the constants without pretending an archival PDF checksum exists.

## Why commercial pursuit does not pass

There is real pain and a paid category. [Kodaris release KOR-154](https://www.kodaris.com/content/releases/kor-154-release) records an actual excessive-carton-count defect and specific checks. [Paccurate pricing](https://paccurate.io/pricing) offers cartonization from $249/month, including a separate enterprise analysis category. [Cycle Labs](https://cyclelabs.io/blog/off-the-shelf-testing-for-complex-wms-landscapes/) supplies WMS testing. These support a shipping-engineering/QA budget owner; they do not establish this specific failure's frequency or willingness to purchase an extra $500 audit.

The defensibility problem is substantive. [Prior metamorphic research](https://mice.cs.columbia.edu/getTechreport.php?format=pdf&techreportID=1550) already uses bin packing; [Packvium](https://packvium.com/) documents independent validation and fixture comparison; [CycleData](https://cyclelabs.io/blog/design-wms-test-data-to-expose-hidden-risks/) addresses generated WMS test inputs. Independently found competitors further narrow the wedge: [CartonPilot](https://cartonpilot.com/cartonization-api) offers a free audit and cost/DIM-aware API, while [Sendflex](https://www.sendflex.com/resources/articles/parcel-simulators-proving-intelligent-cartonization-roi) offers historical-order what-if simulation. Their marketing savings are not independently verified, and no head-to-head was executed.

The constructive fee-linked witness is narrower than those generic services and clears the novelty floor. But public tariff maintenance, adapters and copied test code do not yet explain durable value against established integrators. A permissioned real-regression corpus could help, but no access or cross-client reuse rights exist. Under the frozen rubric, an unacquired hypothetical corpus cannot supply the missing defensibility point.

## Rights and bounded next experiment

Exact py3dbp 1.1.2 source metadata declares MIT, although its distribution omits a license file; preserve the repository's copyright/license notice when redistributing source. Hypothesis 6.168.0 ships MPL-2.0; sortedcontainers 2.4.0 ships Apache-2.0. The inspected exact-release records and hashes are preserved. Unmodified dependency installation and original synthetic fixtures are compatible with offline evaluation. Do not redistribute the carrier PDF or pool customer data without the corresponding permission.

The proposed experiment is adequately specified: three consenting integrator/shipping teams, 21 days, 24 total hours at $100/hour plus $300 cash, maximum $2,700 fully loaded. Recruitment and integration consume that same budget. Compare authorized order/catalog/contract inputs with current team QA; require two complete datasets, one consequential missed regression, two hours saved per audit, and a $500 repeat-audit acceptance. Kill on inaccessible data, no confirmed incremental finding, more than eight integration hours per team, exhausted caps, or no paid-repeat acceptance. The [actual partner directory](https://paccurate.io/partners/) makes recruitment concrete, but does not imply relationships or responses. No outreach was performed or authorized by this review.

What would change this judgment is evidence of repeated incremental findings across independent current engines/workflows, together with a credible permissioned corpus or specialized adapter/rule-maintenance advantage that buyers cannot cheaply obtain from existing suppliers. Raising scores merely because the prototype worked would be criteria shopping.
