# Additional parent screens: rejected before prototyping

Observed 2026-09-18. These are exploratory proposals, not scored pursuit candidates or completed license audits.

1. **KiCad + ngspice + robust parameter search for substitute-component qualification.** Buyer hypothesis: electronics engineering teams replacing unavailable or expensive parts. [Circuit Mind](https://www.circuitmind.io/) already sells component selection, schematic generation and multi-objective BOM optimization. Its [component sourcing demonstration](https://www.circuitmind.io/resource-center/optimized-component-selection-and-sourcing-in-5-minutes) directly covers the proposed paid workflow. [IEEE PELS Circuit AI](https://www.ieee-pels.org/magazine/circuit-ai-for-bill-of-materials-switching-loss-optimization-capacitor-rms-estimation-and-more/) also describes reference-design component alternatives. Generic proposal rejected: no specific measured advantage, simulation models cannot establish physical substitute reliability, and input model rights would require validation.
2. **IfcOpenShell + geometry processing + integer optimization for reclaimed timber-to-design allocation.** Buyer hypothesis: circular-construction consultants. [Concular's primary account](https://concular.de/wiederverwendung-von-baumaterialien-zirkularitaet-im-bauwesen/) already describes automatic matching and digital material records. The [Green-AI pilot](https://www.green-ai-hub.de/pilotprojekte/pilotprojekt-concular) explicitly adds tolerances, minimal adaptations and whole-building recommendations to a Revit workflow. [The 2024 timber case study](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/14780771241234447) already optimizes reclaimed-stock assignment for environmental impact and transport. Generic proposal rejected: substantial direct overlap; a synthetic greedy-baseline win would not establish incremental value over existing tools.

These screens helped avoid more prototypes whose broad mechanisms already exist. No claim that an incumbent's advertised performance is independently verified, and no claim that these whole markets lack opportunity.
