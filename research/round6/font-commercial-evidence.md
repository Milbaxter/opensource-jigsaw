# Font-release witness generation: primary workflow evidence

Observed18September2026. No candidate implementation, evaluation or score in this note.

- [ArrowType's Diffenator2 issue72](https://github.com/googlefonts/diffenator2/issues/72), July2023: the practitioner wants a wordlist exercising all OpenType features for each font version and describes cumbersome per-line feature declarations and uncertainty about whether the supplied proof cases ran. This is a concrete recurring test-authoring task, not a customer commitment to a new product.
- [Diffenator2's README](https://github.com/googlefonts/diffenator2/blob/main/README.md) explicitly limits coverage for abbreviations, names and optional features without custom wordlists. It already includes structural tree comparison, visual diffing, variable-axis sampling and CI integration. Its linked successor Diffenator3 must also be inspected. A new tool cannot claim visual diffing or CI as new capabilities; the possible contribution is executable witnesses for otherwise unexercised behavior.
- [Marc Foley's own business biography](https://www.pdfdiffer.com/author.html) describes contracting for Google Fonts to develop font-update testing software and earlier font engineering at Dalton Maag. This is firsthand supplier evidence of paid work in the exact category. The page is undated and no contract amount was verified.
- [Dave Crossland's buyer-origin Google Fonts contract posting](https://typedrawers.com/discussion/2054/google-fonts-freelance-font-engineering-remote-contract-summer-2017), March2017, offered a compensated three-month remote engagement focused on font quality, bug fixes and update automation with fonttools. It is historical evidence, not an active2026vacancy or current budget.
- [Content Factory font-development services](https://contentfactory.in/font-development.html) list OpenType feature engineering and quality assurance in a current service offering. No fee for a standalone QA engagement was verified. Retail font prices or a full custom-typeface quote should not be presented as the price of this proposed witness generator.

The proposed purchase would be a reviewed release-regression report or CI integration for foundries/font engineering teams. Behavioral change is not automatically an error; intentional substitutions, renamed glyph IDs, script/language settings and feature flags need explicit handling. No outreach, sale or engagement has occurred. Exact source/version rights and a strongest-baseline comparison remain prerequisites to any validation claim.
