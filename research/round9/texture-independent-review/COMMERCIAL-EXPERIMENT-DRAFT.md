# Conditional commercial experiment: bounded texture-allocation delivery

Drafted 18 September 2026 before technical results. **No score, recommendation to launch, assumed technical pass, outreach, purchase, or customer access.** This plan becomes eligible for separate approval only if the frozen technical comparison demonstrates its stated advantage and independent review finds the essential bridge and rights usable. A technical win alone does not establish the buyer-pain, distribution, defensibility, or economic gates in rubric v2.

## Observed evidence and counterevidence

The [buyer-originated Upwork posting](https://www.upwork.com/freelance-jobs/apply/Quick-Task-Compress-Optimize-Heavy-GLB-Models-for-Shopify-Remodeling_~022091785499043108499/) advertises $50 for three large GLBs and requested texture/geometry optimization for Shopify. It is a one-time advertised budget, not evidence that this project paid, that texture allocation alone solves it, or that the buyer has recurring demand. Its public page was still readable today, but current hiring availability is unverified. The requested Draco/possible geometry changes and Shopify target exceed the present texture-only, pinned-Three.js study.

[SpatialPack](https://www.spatialpack.dev/) advertises $39/month Pro, automated recipes and rendered checks; the previously captured commercial source screen records its $15/model catalog offer with ten-model minimum, or $12/model at 25+. These are vendor offers, not observed purchase or performance evidence. The $39 self-service alternative can be cheaper than even one proposed $50 batch. The [GLBStudio](https://glb.studio/) $29/month offer is additional price pressure. Do not justify a large integration fee with unrelated enterprise optimization-suite pricing.

The conditional experiment tests a **small paid delivery service** for a buyer who needs an independently demonstrated allocation advantage at an explicit byte ceiling. Reports, presets, local operation, and visual checks already exist; they are not themselves the differentiation. No measured human-time or perceived-quality benefit currently exists. The strongest accessible automatic comparator, competent presets, and rendered greedy remain baselines; a paid vendor's full product is not presumed inferior to its bounded public API frontier.

## Buyer, channel, and permission boundary

Target budget owner: the owner or technical production lead of a small agency maintaining a custom Three.js/WebGL product viewer, with customer-owned GLBs, a near-term release, and at least one genuine next revision/batch. Only accept a deployment whose actual viewer supports the selected texture extensions. Exclude native Shopify/Quick Look/USDZ delivery promises, geometry-reduction requirements, animation, unsupported materials, and cases where the reference already meets budget. This narrower segment is a proposed sample, not an observed population of twelve available buyers.

Use **Upwork's ordinary proposal route to currently open, matching jobs**, manually selected; no harvested emails, unsolicited off-platform contact, automated mass applications, or presumed permission to post promotions in technical forums. Official [proposal instructions](https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/211062998-How-to-submit-a-proposal-on-Upwork) describe this route and prohibit repeated unwanted contact. Keep precontract communication on the platform under its [terms](https://www.upwork.com/legal). A public listing permits reading, not taking or redistributing a buyer's files. This draft authorizes no contact.

Before launch the user must designate an accountable human service owner with an eligible platform account and explicitly authorize proposals, terms, receipt of customer files, and spending. Neither account eligibility nor a recruitable pool is established by this research. If these cannot be arranged within the cap, HOLD the acquisition experiment; do not claim its distribution gate is already satisfied.

Prospective sampling: in the first two working hours inspect at most 30 current public listings over at most three days, using fixed queries “GLB optimization”, “glTF compression”, and “Three.js product viewer”. Log date, URL, actual scope, budget, and inclusion/exclusion reason. Proceed only with at least eight independently owned, in-scope opportunities. Send at most twelve individually relevant proposals, no more than one per buyer; use earliest qualifying postings first, without filtering on apparent enthusiasm. Target two paying organizations; take at most two. If fewer than eight qualifying opportunities exist, stop for inadequate channel density rather than invent another audience after outcomes. This stage determines whether the proposed sample actually exists.

## Fixed offer and delivered artifact

Offer **$50 total for one batch of up to three eligible static GLBs**, one supported viewer target, one agreed byte ceiling per asset, and one correction limited to defects in the promised deliverable. This deliberately faces the observed $50/three-model price. Disclose that $39/month SpatialPack is a credible self-service alternative and that the candidate is experimental. Do not promise perceptual equivalence or platform compatibility beyond the verified target. No discount, free custom optimization, hidden setup fee, or conditional testimonial request. A supported, already-good asset is not manufactured into paid work.

Deliver the candidate and best available baseline files, exact byte/codec manifests, reproducible command/configuration, same-view comparison images, numerical error report, and a concise compatibility/limitation note. A buyer can choose the baseline. Production publication remains the buyer's decision. Record actual acceptance separately from numerical error. Complete a tiny format/rights eligibility check before agreeing a funded milestone; no custom model engineering outside the fixed scope. If the service cannot fulfill that scope, decline or refund per the agreed terms and count it as failure, not successful demand.

After the first delivery, offer the **same $50 terms** for a genuinely changed next batch or revision. At most four batches/12 assets total across the two organizations. Do not count rerunning an unchanged file, a refundable expression of interest, or the founder funding a friendly buyer as repeat demand. A second paid order within fourteen days is an early repeat-use proxy, not retention.

## Resources and fully loaded economics

Duration: **14 calendar days** from approved start, with a hard **20 person-hour** cap. Responsible owner: the user-designated human service owner; one engineer may perform the technical work. Until designated, this is an unstaffed proposal.

| Work | Maximum hours |
|---|---:|
| Eligibility/deployment/rights preparation and customer-facing scope | 3 |
| Channel screening, proposals, qualification, and sales administration | 4 |
| Packaging and customer-specific integration (no new product UI) | 6 |
| Running/reviewing/delivering up to four batches | 4 |
| All support, corrections, and refunds administration | 2 |
| Final economic/evidence review | 1 |
| **Total** | **20** |

Prospective fully loaded labor assumption: **$75/hour**, explicitly an internal hurdle rate rather than an observed wage or saving. Value all 20 hours: **$1,500**. Cash/compute cap: **$250**, comprising at most $50 proposal credits/platform acquisition spend, $60 compute/storage, $40 optional independent commercial comparator subscription, and $100 fees/refunds/contingency. Total maximum incremental study resource cost: **$1,750**, plus separately disclosed prior sunk research. No revenue offsets this authorization cap, and the money is not spent now. Local compute has a declared $1/node-hour accounting charge plus actual incremental storage/network costs; this is an assumption for the experiment, not a cloud quote. Record raw wall/device time so another cost rate can be applied.

Current [Connects pricing](https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/211062898-Understanding-and-using-Connects) is $0.15 each, with variable proposal requirements; do not assume all twelve proposals fit the cap. The [freelancer service fee](https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/211062538-Learn-about-the-Freelancer-Service-Fee) is 0–15% by contract; record the actual shown rate and plan at 15%. Do not omit the buyer's separately shown platform charges from pricing feedback.

At $50 and a 15% seller fee, net receipts are $42.50. To produce at least $20 contribution per batch with $3 compute/storage, total routine human handling must be **no more than 15.6 minutes at $75/hour**; set the prospective target to 15 minutes. Include intake, review, delivery, and amortized corrections in this routine handling measure. This is an unproven stringent target, not a claimed saving. A single hour of manual optimization makes the offer unprofitable. Implementation engineering is reported separately and remains fully counted in the $1,750 study cost. Even a successful four-batch pilot cannot repay development: maximum gross pilot revenue is only $200.

Loaded acquisition cost includes screening/proposal/interview time at $75/hour plus credits, divided by actual paying organizations—not replies. Target at most **$60 per paying organization**, payable from at most three observed-$20-contribution batches; the full screening cap might make this fail. Do not amortize acquisition over an invented annual lifetime. Any unused time allowance stays unused.

## Baseline, measurement, and prospective decisions

Before customer work, require the frozen public benchmark's actual advantage, all integrity controls, and inspected resource measurements. If the demonstrated advantage needs incompatible viewers, unavailable rights, or routine handling/compute obviously above this offer's break-even limit, do not initiate recruitment. Use the same supported export boundary; a general engineering engagement is not silently substituted.

For consenting customer inputs, keep each source unchanged and compare candidate, competent presets/greedy, and the strongest lawful accessible comparator on the same declared target, byte ceiling, and supported material semantics. Register camera/light review conditions and customer acceptance requirements before presenting outputs. Record bytes, error, runtime, failures, hands-on minutes, support, fees, and customer choice. Show randomized A/B labels only with agreement that both are generated alternatives and disclose their identities after selection; do not misrepresent either as an untouched original. Private input results need not be published.

Continue to a separately budgeted next study only if **all** hold:

- At least eight eligible opportunities were found within the screening cap and two independent organizations paid the unchanged $50 first-batch price. Record funded milestones and released receipts separately; only released payment counts as earned revenue.
- Both buyers accept usable delivered output in the specified viewer. At least two assets from two organizations retain the preregistered practical numerical advantage over the strongest baseline, and no integrity/compatibility failure is concealed. Human acceptance is newly observed, not inferred from error.
- At least one organization places and pays for one genuine changed second batch within the window. Record any second-batch delivery/payment still pending as inconclusive, not success.
- Actual routine batch contribution reaches $20 after fees, measured labor and compute, without shifting work into unrecorded integration/support. Report first and repeat batches individually. Total acquisition cost is at most $60 per paying organization, and payback requires no more than three batches at the observed contribution.
- At least one buyer's next genuine revision exercises a reusable, explicitly permitted configuration or regression test, and that reuse is observed to avoid redoing identified setup work. Measure the work; do not assume a durable data advantage.

Kill or HOLD this service proposition if any budget/time limit is reached; no paid first order appears by day 10; adequate matching jobs are unavailable; buyers prefer the available $39 self-service alternative with no reason to buy the narrow advantage; both buyers require out-of-scope geometry/AR work; ordinary presets already meet their need; manual work destroys the contribution target; no legitimate repeat order arrives by day 14; or input/rights access is denied. Preserve partial positive evidence but do not soften thresholds or present “interested” responses as conversion. This small study cannot estimate market-wide conversion, long-term retention or profitability.

## Durable value and acquisition of evidence

The potential repeatable asset is a customer-permitted corpus of renderer/material regression cases, stable per-catalog compatibility configurations, and reproducible allocation decisions across actual revisions. These are hypotheses about reduced future integration/support work, not a proprietary algorithm or an already-owned dataset. CP-SAT and encoders remain commodity tools; competitors can implement the same mechanism.

Acquire only contractual rights necessary to process each customer-owned asset and return outputs. Default: private local processing, no public upload, no training use, no resale or retention beyond an agreed deletion date. Ask separately, without tying delivery or price to consent, whether deidentified numeric failure metadata and a minimal synthetic regression reproduction may be retained. Raw customer models or distinctive textures require separate express retention/publication permission. A hash is provenance, not permission. If consent is denied, delete inputs as agreed and keep only permitted invoice/accounting records; do not claim a data moat. Do not incorporate or redistribute the restricted SpatialPack core into the service; comparator use must remain within its exact inspected research/internal-use license and any paid service's terms.

Potential defensibility is only supported if a permitted regression/configuration artifact actually transfers across a changed second batch and produces measured integration/support benefit. If every batch requires bespoke labor, this is a low-price freelance job, not an evidenced repeatable integration business.
